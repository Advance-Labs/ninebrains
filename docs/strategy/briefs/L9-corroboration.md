# L9 — Corroboration and partners

**Model:** Sonnet. **Workstream:** [W10](../workstreams/W10-external-corroboration.md).
**Risk tier:** Standard; **Critical** for anything published under a partner's name.

The program's biggest weakness is not features. It is that almost everything written about
Ninebrains was written by Advance Labs. Your job is to change the ratio honestly.

## Traps specific to this lane

- **Ten substantive independent references beat a hundred near-duplicate "best tools" posts
  we wrote ourselves.** Generating those posts is forbidden, not discouraged.
- **At least three case studies must be authored or co-authored by the users.** The exact
  may / may-not-edit list is in
  [W10](../workstreams/W10-external-corroboration.md#what-we-may-and-may-not-edit-in-a-partner-authored-piece)
  — use that list, not a remembered version of it. In short: we may fix factual errors about
  Ninebrains with the partner's sign-off and copyedit with their final approval; we may
  never add a claim they did not make, remove a criticism, change their verdict, or publish
  before they have approved the final text.
- **Give reviewers the benchmark kit AND explicit permission to publish failures.** A
  reviewer who feels managed writes a review nobody believes.
- **A submission that makes us look bad gets published.** Decide that now, in writing,
  before the first one arrives.
- **CASL.** Contacts only from a company's own site or their own listing. Log where, when,
  and why relevant, every time.
- **Prospect-facing email copy carries zero em dashes, subject lines included.** This
  includes the templates you write into the workstream doc as examples.
